# Jharkhand_Minerals_Qgis
This project explores the relationship between mineral wealth and local development in Jharkhand, India, using Geographic Information System (GIS) techniques.
Jharkhand is one of India's major mineral-rich states. However, the distribution of mineral resources and the benefits of mining-related revenue may vary across districts.
The project uses QGIS to visualise district-level data and examine the spatial distribution of District Mineral Foundation (DMF) funds across the 24 districts of Jharkhand.
#Mineral_rich_states_gephi
India's mineral economy is an important part of its industrial development, infrastructure, employment generation, and regional economic structure. However, mineral wealth is not necessarily distributed equally, and the relationship between resource extraction and local development requires careful investigation.
This project explores India's mineral economy through a combination of network analysis and geospatial analysis. It aims to examine connections among mineral resources, mineral belts, states, and related economic or institutional entities, while considering their geographical distribution.
The project uses Gephi for network visualisation and analysis, with a broader aim of understanding how India's mineral economy is spatially and structurally organised.
